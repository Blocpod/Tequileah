Questions

Straight answers.

Who is TequiLeah for?

Builders and creatives in South Florida who are already making something real: engineers, designers, AI builders, hardware people, filmmakers, and solo founders. You don't need a company yet. You need work in progress.

Where is it, and when is it open?

The studio is in Hialeah, Miami, and it runs during the day. Members get the exact address and hours when they're accepted.

How do investor introductions work?

When your work is ready, we introduce you in person to investors whose focus matches what you're building. No mass email lists and no cold decks.

Do I need funding, a team, or a pitch deck?

No. Show us what you've shipped and what you're working on now. Proof matters more than polish.

What does it cost?

We walk every accepted builder through membership on a short intro call. No surprises.

I'm an investor. How do I get involved?

Join the investor circle. You'll see real work from builders in the room, and we'll make introductions when there's a match. Join the investor circle
